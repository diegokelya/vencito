How it works

From the store shelf to your fridge, in three steps

  1. Snap a photo

    Of the package, with the date in view. If the barcode shows up in the photo, it reads that too.

  2. Vencito fills in the rest

    It reads the date, suggests a name and category, and looks the barcode up on Open Food Facts. You review and save.

  3. Get reminded in time

    One notification a day, at the hour you choose, with what is about to expire: by default 30, 15, 7, 3 and 1 day before.

Privacy

Your data stays on your iPhone

Vencito has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no ads.

All on-device

Products, photos and settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad.

AI without the cloud

Text, barcodes and Apple Intelligence recognition are processed on the device.

One optional piece of data leaves

To look up a product, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts. You can turn it off.

Local reminders

Notifications are scheduled by the iPhone itself. They never go through a server.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost?

It’s free, with nothing locked. If you find it useful, you can buy me a coffee with an optional tip in Settings.

Do I need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence?

No. Without it, the app reads the date and name with Apple’s text recognition. AI adds the category and reads tricky packages better.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Only the barcode product lookup needs the internet.

Can I move my data to another phone?

Yes. In Settings you can export a file with all your products (with or without photos) and import it on another device.

What if the date shows only month and year?

The app uses the last day of that month, which is how packages are meant to be read.
